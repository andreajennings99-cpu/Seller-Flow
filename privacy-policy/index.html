1. Introduction

Welcome to Seller Flow. Seller Flow is a productivity and business management application designed to help small business owners organize products, manage listings, track orders, monitor revenue and expenses, plan daily activities, and manage physical and digital products.

This Privacy Policy explains how Seller Flow collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and deletes personal information when users access its mobile application, web interface, and administrative services.

By using Seller Flow, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it separately.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, Seller Flow may process the following categories of information.

Account and Profile Information: Email addresses; account names and business names; authentication and account identifiers; account preferences and settings.

Business and Product Information: Product names, descriptions, photographs, and prices; inventory quantities and product costs; digital product files and external download links; product categories, keywords, and listing information; marketplace and sales channel information.

Order and Customer Information: Order identifiers and purchase records; customer names and contact information entered by sellers; shipping and fulfillment details; digital delivery information; customer follow-up notes.

Financial Information: Sales and revenue records; business expenses and transaction fees; product costs and estimated profits; subscription status and billing records.

Productivity Information: Tasks, deadlines, reminders, and schedules; goals, progress, and completion history; focus timer activity and productivity streaks; notes, sketches, uploaded images, and voice recordings.

Technical Information: Depending on the final implementation, Seller Flow may process device information, app activity, diagnostic information, IP addresses, and authentication or security logs needed to operate and protect the service.

Not every category is necessarily collected from every user.

3. How We Use Information

Seller Flow processes information to: Create and manage user accounts; provide productivity planning and business management features; organize products, listings, inventory, and digital downloads; track sales, orders, fulfillment, and expenses; generate business reports and weekly productivity summaries; provide subscription access and account management; deliver requested reminders and notifications; respond to support inquiries; protect accounts and prevent unauthorized activity; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information is processed only for purposes supported by the app's functionality, applicable permissions, and legal requirements.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Seller Flow offers subscription-based access to certain services and features.

Subscription purchases may be processed through Google Play Billing or another payment provider, depending on the platform and the final payment implementation.

Payment providers may independently process payment credentials, billing details, transaction identifiers, and payment status according to their own privacy policies.

Seller Flow may receive transaction confirmations, subscription status, and billing identifiers necessary to manage paid access.

Subscription pricing, renewal terms, cancellation procedures, and applicable refund conditions will be disclosed during the purchase process.

Deleting a Seller Flow account does not necessarily cancel an active subscription managed by an external payment provider. Users should cancel subscriptions through the applicable billing service.

5. Digital Products and File Storage

Seller Flow supports the management of physical and digital products.

Users may upload product images, downloadable files, business documents, and other content. They may also provide external download links.

Seller Flow processes this information to organize product catalogs, manage listings, and support authorized digital delivery.

Uploaded content should be accessible only to authorized users and recipients according to the application's access controls.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights and permissions to upload, store, and distribute their content.

6. Voice Recordings, Images, and Device Permissions

Seller Flow may offer optional voice note recording, image uploads, photographs, sketches, and other multimedia features.

When these features require microphone, camera, or file access, the application will request the relevant permissions as required by the device operating system.

Users may decline optional permissions, although certain features may become unavailable.

The application will process recordings and media for the features users choose to access. Any additional use, such as third-party speech transcription, must be disclosed when implemented.

7. Marketplace Integrations

Seller Flow supports organizing business activity associated with Etsy, Shopify, Amazon, in-person markets, social media, and direct orders.

Users may manually enter or tag information relating to these platforms.

If direct marketplace connections are introduced, Seller Flow may process information authorized by users through the applicable platform's integration or authentication process.

Connected services may have separate privacy policies and data-handling practices.

Seller Flow is not affiliated with or endorsed by these marketplaces solely because their names are supported as sales channels.

8. Information Sharing and Service Providers

Seller Flow may disclose information to service providers when necessary to operate the application, including providers responsible for authentication and account management; application hosting and database storage; file hosting and digital delivery; subscription billing and payment processing; notifications and technical support; security, diagnostics, or analytics, where implemented.

Service providers should receive only the information necessary for their authorized functions and must be subject to appropriate contractual and security safeguards.

Seller Flow may also disclose information when legally required, to protect the service or its users, or when users authorize an integration or transfer.

The use of advertising networks, behavioral advertising, or analytics services has not yet been finalized. Any such processing will require appropriate disclosure and consent where applicable before activation.

9. Data Security

Seller Flow is committed to protecting personal and business information.

Security safeguards appropriate to the application's implementation should include encrypted network communications, authenticated access, restricted administrative permissions, secure file access, and protection against unauthorized account activity.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should protect their account credentials and notify Seller Flow if they suspect unauthorized access.

10. Data Retention

Seller Flow retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested services, maintain accounts, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the application.

The retention period may depend on the category of information, the user's account status, applicable legal requirements, and the storage provider.

Certain transaction or accounting records may need to be retained after account closure to comply with tax, financial, or other legal obligations.

Information that is no longer needed will be deleted or de-identified in accordance with applicable requirements and the application's documented retention procedures.

11. Account and Data Deletion

Seller Flow provides an in-app account deletion option.

Users can request deletion through the account management or settings area of the application.

Users may also request account and associated data deletion by emailing sellerflow3096@gmail.com with the subject "Seller Flow Account Deletion Request".

Users should provide the email address associated with their Seller Flow account. Additional verification may be requested when necessary to prevent unauthorized deletion.

Account deletion includes removing associated personal information and stored user content, subject to limited retention required by applicable law, fraud prevention, security, or legitimate recordkeeping obligations.

Where information must be retained, Seller Flow will restrict its use to the applicable retention purpose.

Account deletion does not automatically remove information independently held by external marketplaces or payment providers.
